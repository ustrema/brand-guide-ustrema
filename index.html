01 · The name

Устрем — drive, impetus, forward rush.

A real Bulgarian word that local buyers recognise instantly, and that sounds smooth and abstract to EU and US buyers, the same trick that works for Sirma and Musala. The name holds half the brand idea by itself. The other half, direction, is what Ustrema sells.

Say it

oo-STREH-mah

BG: у-СТРЕ-ма. Stress on the 2nd syllable.

Write it

Logotype (v2): title-case Ustrema in Manrope 800, with Cyrillic Устрема as the bilingual line. Digital signature version: Ustrema_ (blinking cursor).
Legal: УСТРЕМА ООД / USTREMA LTD.
In running text: Ustrema / Устрема. Never USTREMA in body copy, because it reads as an acronym.

The tension it carries

Power Platform gives every employee устрем. Without direction, that becomes sprawl, data exposure and risk. Ustrema = the drive, plus the direction.

Watch-out: some EN speakers will say "you-strema" or hear "extreme". Put a pronunciation line on the About page and in email signatures during year one. Don't change the spelling.

The research behind the palette

How the palette was chosen

Every chromatic brand colour from the 70 competitors in the research (sites, CSS and screenshots), plotted by hue. Outer bars show the count per 20° band; inner dots are the actual colours. Lettered dots are our candidate signals.

  • Blue 200–239°: 50 colours, the category default, and Microsoft's own.
  • Red/orange 0–39°: 32. The v1 vermilion lived here.
  • Cyan 180–199°: 8, almost all pale sky-blue. Electric cyan on black: nobody.
  • Mint 140–170°: taken by a close local competitor. Avoid.
  • Violet 280–300°: 0. Empty, but trend-loaded.

Strategy first: blend in, bridge, or break?

Conform & own

Go with the blue majority

Blue is the colour most strongly linked to competence in the research, and it's the ecosystem's native language. You trade memorability for instant trust. 50 of 135 competitor colours are blue, and the "best blue" rarely wins a three-way pitch comparison.

Scheme: B · Ultramarine
Bridge

Trust language, own dialect

Deep indigo or plum bases read as serious as navy at a glance; the accent moves to violet or cyan. Easy for conservative buyers, more ownable than blue. Risk: violet and cyan are both "AI/tech" defaults.

Schemes: I · Indigo & Iris, P · Plum & Cyan
Break (recommended)

Be the one that isn't blue

Earn trust through restraint (monochrome base, strong typography, real findings), not through hue. Spend the colour budget on one accent nobody in the category uses. Research suggests colour fit to the brand matters more than the specific hue.

Schemes: O · Fuchsia, M · Monochrome, X · Oxblood, Y · Saffron

Psychological placement map

x-axis: category-conforming → distinctive, measured against the 135-colour competitor map. y-axis: calm/trust → energy, from the psychological associations of hue, saturation and value.

The category crowds two places: blue-calm (the trust default) and orange/red-energetic (the CTA default). The upper right, distinctive and energetic but still credible, is empty. That is where an "устрем" brand should sit, and where Fuchsia lands.

Colour–trait links: blue → competence, red → excitement, purple and black → sophistication (Labrecque & Milne, 2012, Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science). Colour–brand fit matters more than any single hue (Bottomley & Doyle, 2006, Marketing Theory). Positions are analyst judgement, not measurement.

10 · Graphic language

Slash, cursor, lock-on.

Three devices carry the brand without stock imagery. All three come from the logo exploration: the 45° slash from the spear tip, the cursor from the inspo wordmark, and the lock-on frame from exploration 11. The fourth is the finding card, real-looking tenant data that acts as our "photography".

45° Slash pattern

Backgrounds, banners, section breaks. Always 45° (the tip angle). Max one signal slash per composition.

Cursor

Scan_
Ends headlines and CTAs in digital, blinks once on load. It means "live, building, ready". Never more than one per screen.

Iconography

Line icons, 1.75 px stroke on a 24 px grid, square caps, 18° forward slant where direction matters. Never use Microsoft product icons (Power Apps, Power BI…) as decoration. Where a product must be identified, use its name in text, per Microsoft's guidelines.

Imagery

Do
  • Product-like UI compositions: findings, environment maps, dashboards (anonymised)
  • Real team photos, natural light, Sofia office/city context, shot as candid documentary
  • Duotone treatment (Ink + Paper) for any photo used in hero areas
  • Diagrams that explain: environment routing, DLP groups, ALM pipelines
Don't
  • Stock handshakes, people pointing at glass screens, VR headsets
  • Glowing network nodes, blue globes, "digital brain" AI art
  • Microsoft's four-colour squares or product icons as decoration
  • Generic AI-generated people

15 · Partner & trademark guardrails

Look like a partner, not like Microsoft.

Do
  • Before designation, use plain text only: "Member of the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program"
  • After designation: "Solutions Partner for AI Business Solutions" badge (from Partner Center), unaltered, ≥130 px wide, in the footer or on the partner page only, never combined with our logo
  • Expect the designation in ~12–13 months (the usage-growth metric needs a trailing 12 months of PAL-attributed usage), so design credentials to work badge-less
  • Use full product names with "Microsoft" on first mention per page: "Microsoft Power Platform". No abbreviations (M365, D365) in public copy
  • Use ® / ™ attribution in the footer: "Microsoft, Power Platform … are trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies."
  • Show individual certifications by exam code and name
Don't
  • Use the Microsoft logo, four-square symbol, product icons or Segoe as brand elements
  • Put product names in our name, domain, logo or offer names (e.g. "PowerScan")
  • Imply endorsement ("Microsoft-approved", "official Microsoft …")
  • Use Microsoft's exact blues as our primary colour
  • Claim a designation, specialization or "Gold/Silver" (retired 2022) status, or say "official" / "certified partner"
  • Use Power Apps / Power BI / Copilot product icons decoratively, on service cards or as a "tech stack" strip (allowed only in diagrams and documentation)
  • Use product colours (Power BI yellow, Power Apps purple) as brand colours
